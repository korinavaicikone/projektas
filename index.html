The game, in four moves

One drawing.
Many plot twists.

Inspired by the French drawing game Cadavre Exquis, this is a simple way to make something together - without worrying about getting it right.

01 / BEGIN

Start drawing

Everyone gets paper, a prompt, and a chance to start something. No overthinking needed.

02 / PASS

Pass it on

Your drawing travels to another participant. They take it somewhere you probably didn't expect.

03 / BUILD

Keep it moving

Drawings move between people, gathering new ideas, details and unexpected directions.

04 / REVEAL

Enjoy the chaos

Reveal the finished creations and see what your team made together. The results can get weird.
